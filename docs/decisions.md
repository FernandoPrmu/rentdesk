# RentDesk — Business rule decisions

Rules the spec (`docs/spec.md` v1.1) does not define, chosen during development. Please
review each one; any of them can be changed. "Where" points to the code or setting that
implements it.

**Client review (2026-10-09):** rules 1, 2, 4, 5, 7 and 13 were changed and rules 16–19 added
(advance payments and security deposits). The new requirement IDs are in `docs/spec.md`
section 18.

**Task 5 (2026-10-10):** rules 20-27 (tickets and the daily job). The client agreed rules 20-24 and 27
as proposed, and rule 23 with the addition that became rule 25.

**Task 6a (2026-10-11):** rules 28-32 (meter entry and the owner's review), agreed by the client as proposed.

**Task 6b (2026-10-11):** rules 33-38 (invoice PDFs, letterhead, camera safety). The client decided rule 33
(English only) and agreed rules 34-38 as proposed, including keeping removed logos and letterheads (rule 35) and no
PAID stamp on invoice PDFs (rule 34; proof of payment will be a separate receipt PDF in the payments task).

**Task 7 (2026-10-11):** rules 39-46 (payments). The client agreed the split (rule 39), the ageing of bills with a
slip waiting (rule 45), adding reallocation now (rule 43) and leaving the virus scan for later (rule 41); the other
rules are proposed for review.

| 1 | Billing cycle | **Monthly** on the day of the month of the first billing date (client decision, 2026-10-09). When that day does not exist (29th–31st) the last day of the month is used, and the next month goes back to the original day, always counted from the first billing date, so there is no drift: 31 Jan → 28 Feb → 31 Mar → 30 Apr. Cycle *n* covers the days from cycle *n − 1*'s date to the day before its own date. `billing_day` holds that day; the old `cycle_length_days` is gone (tickets keep `cycle_length_days` as the real number of days of their cycle). | Client review of rule 1 ("every N days"). Customers think in months; counting from the first date avoids the 28th creeping in after February. | `src/lib/agreements/cycle-calendar.ts`; `app.cycle_date`, `app.cycle_in_progress`, `app.cycle_days` (migration 0017) |
| 2 | First billing date | The start date may be in the past (rentals already running), but the first billing date must be today or later. Suggested: one month after the start (rolled forward by whole months, never in the past). No ticket is ever opened for a period before the first billing date. | Owners migrate existing rentals without billing periods that were already settled outside RentDesk. | `rental_agreements.first_billing_date`; `rpc_assign_machine`; `defaultFirstBillingDate` |
| 3 | Terms changes | A pricing change applies from the cycle after the one in progress. Copies made today keep the old terms, and so does every ticket already open. | Spec AGR-02 / 11.3 say "from the next billing period"; this is the strictest reading, so no amount changes after the customer started using a period. | `rpc_update_agreement_terms`; `agreement_terms_history` |
| 4 | Machine return | A machine **can be returned with unpaid invoices** (client decision). The owner enters the closing readings; the system issues the final invoice through the billing engine (rule 5) and the machine becomes Available at once. Unpaid invoices stay on the customer's account: the customer can still sign in, see the balance and send payment slips. Meter requests still open for earlier cycles are cancelled with the reason "Billed in the final invoice" (their months are in it). A return is **blocked only while a meter reading waits for the owner's review** (Pending owner review, also when overdue), so the final bill starts from confirmed numbers. Reassign follows the same rules in one transaction. | Client review of rule 4: owners take machines back from customers who stopped paying. | `rpc_return_machine`, `rpc_reassign_machine`, `app.agreement_return_blockers` (0017) |
| 5 | Final invoice on return | The owner chooses at return time: **prorated** (default) or **full month**. The invoice covers every whole cycle since the last confirmed reading plus the cycle in progress. Prorated: commitment × days used ÷ **real days of that cycle** (28–31), included copies prorated the same way, excess on actual usage. Days used = return date − start of the cycle + 1 (the return day counts). Issued at once (the owner typed the readings), due after the agreement's days to pay; a total of zero (fully covered by credits) is marked paid. | Spec 11.4 says "final invoice per the owner's rule"; client decisions on real days and the return day. | `finalCycle`; `buildReturnInvoice`; `app.final_cycle`, `app.verify_final_invoice` |
| 6 | Rounding | Amounts are whole cents; normal invoices never need rounding (whole copies × cents rate). Only proration rounds: money **half up to the cent**, prorated included copies **half up to a whole copy**. | The spec defines no rounding; half up is the common commercial rule and is neutral between owner and customer. | `src/lib/billing/arithmetic.ts` |
| 7 | Late fee | Optional. A flat amount, added **once** per invoice when it is not fully paid by due date + grace period. Not charged while a payment slip waits for verification, or while the invoice is disputed. Which amount (LATE-01): the **agreement's** setting first (*Use owner default* / *Custom amount* / *No late fee*), then the **owner's** settings (Settings › Billing: on/off, amount, grace days), then the platform default. A custom amount is charged even when the owner's late fee is off; grace days always come from the owner (else platform). The agreement setting is a versioned term (from the next cycle, kept in the terms history, snapshot on each ticket). | Spec 8.3 / PAY-13 say "optional, after a grace period"; per-agreement override is a client decision. | `resolveLateFee`, `lateFeeDue` in `src/lib/billing/late-fee.ts`; `late_fee_mode`/`late_fee_cents` on agreements, terms history and tickets |
| 8 | Usage anomalies | Compared per cycle with the median of the last 6 confirmed cycles (needs at least 3): **HIGH** above 200 % and at least 200 copies above; **LOW** below 50 % and at least 200 copies below; **ZERO** when no counter printed anything. One idle counter (e.g. colour not used) is not flagged on its own. | Spec 6.4 asks to warn about unusual usage but gives no thresholds. The median ignores one odd month; the 200-copy minimum stops tiny volumes from raising alarms. | `src/lib/billing/anomaly.ts` (constants) |
| 9 | Counter rollover | Usage = (counter max − previous) + 1 + current (999,999 → 0 counts one copy), only when the machine has a counter maximum. | Spec 11.2: "the system adds the rollover". | `src/lib/billing/usage.ts` |
| 10 | Rollover guard | A lower reading counts as a rollover only when plausible: not above the HIGH anomaly threshold (or, with less than 3 cycles of history, at most half the counter maximum). Otherwise it is refused as "lower than the previous reading" so the customer re-enters it. An accepted rollover is always flagged, and the owner must confirm it before the invoice is issued (stored on the reading). | A mistyped lower number would otherwise bill close to a million copies. | `counterUsage`; `rpc_confirm_meter_submission(p_rollover_confirmed)` |
| 11 | Previous reading | The previous reading is the newest of: the initial reading, the last confirmed reading, a baseline the owner recorded. On equal times: baseline, then confirmed reading, then initial reading. | Spec 6.4 / 11.8 (baselines after a meter reset); the tie rule only makes results deterministic. | `previousReading`; `app.last_known_reading` |
| 12 | Several cycles in one invoice | A reading covers every cycle since the last confirmed reading; commitment and included copies are multiplied by that number. | Spec 6.4 / 11.6; counting from the last confirmed reading makes skipped cycles impossible to miss. | `rpc_submit_meter_reading` check; `cyclesCovered` |
| 13 | Credits | Estimated charges are credited first (spec 11.6), then customer credits **oldest first, added automatically to every new invoice** (client decision; advances, overpayments, money kept from cancelled invoices). Each CREDIT line names its credit; a draft reserves what it uses, so two invoices never use the same money. The owner sees the credits in review and on the return form and **can remove one before confirming** (e.g. to refund it instead): it stays available and the next credit fills in. Removing and adding back are written to the audit log. A credit never makes an invoice negative; what does not fit stays available. | Client review of rule 13 (was: applied only when the owner decides). | `calculateInvoice({ credits, creditsExcluded })`; `rpc_set_invoice_credit`; `app.verify_invoice_credits`, `app.available_credits` |
| 14 | One calculation | All amounts are calculated by one billing engine (TypeScript). The database stores its result only when the inputs are the ticket's own facts and the arithmetic adds up; it never calculates a second time. | Two implementations could one day disagree; one cannot. | `src/lib/billing`; migration 0015 |
| 15 | Stage defaults | Payment due 7 days after confirmation; stage deadlines as in spec 5.4; lockout after 5 wrong passwords for 15 minutes; idle sign-out after 30 minutes, maximum session 12 hours. | Spec 16.3 leaves the defaults open; all are settings the admin can change. | `platform_settings` |
| 16 | Advance payment | Money paid before any invoice, recorded on the assign form ("Money received upfront": amount, date received — past dates allowed — method, reference, note). It becomes a customer credit of kind ADVANCE linked to the agreement and is taken off the next invoices automatically (rule 13). | Client decision E (DEP-02); spec 11.5 "owner can record an advance payment as credit". | `app.record_upfront_money`; `credits.agreement_id`, `received_on`, `method`, `reference` |
| 17 | Security deposit | Recorded the same way, but **held separately**: it is never a credit and never pays a normal bill. Shown as "Deposit held: Rs. X" on the agreement, the customer profile and the customer's Machines tab. Several entries per agreement are allowed. The ledger (`deposit_transactions`: received, paid an invoice, refunded, kept) is append-only and can never go below zero. | Client decision E (DEP-01). | `deposit_transactions`, `agreement_deposit_balances` (0017) |
| 18 | Deposit settlement | On return (or later, from the agreement page, while it is still held) the owner splits the deposit: **pay unpaid bills** (oldest due first, each as an accepted payment of method *From security deposit*; a paid invoice closes its ticket), **refund** (date, method, reference) and **keep** (reason required). Pay + refund + keep must equal the deposit held, always; all of it in one transaction, audited, the customer notified. Only the agreement's own invoices can be paid from its deposit, and not one with a slip waiting or a dispute. The owner may also keep holding the deposit at return; such deposits are listed under "Deposits to settle" on the dashboard and the customer profile. | Client decisions E and 2 (DEP-03, DEP-04). Bank cash deposits keep the method DEPOSIT; SECURITY_DEPOSIT is a separate method so reports never mix them. | `app.apply_deposit_settlement`, `rpc_settle_deposit`; `src/lib/deposits/settlement.ts` |
| 19 | Retried returns | A return carries a key made when the form opens; a retried request (double tap, lost connection) returns the first result instead of failing or billing twice. | CLAUDE.md idempotency rule. | `rental_agreements.return_idempotency_key` |
| 20 | Stage deadlines | **Day-based stages** (meter reading, payment) fall due at **00:00 Asia/Colombo** on the day: the meter deadline is 00:00 on day 5 after the ticket opened, so the day-5 run marks it overdue; the customer sees "Send by" day 4. Payment is overdue from 00:00 the day after the due date. **Hour-based stages** (owner review of a reading or a slip) count hours from when the stage started (`stage_entered_at`) and fire on the first daily run after the threshold, with **one hour of lookahead** for Vercel's hour-long window (so with one run a day an hour-based reminder can be up to a day late). Each run sends **one** reminder per ticket, the latest one due, never a backlog of missed ones. | Spec 5.4 gives days and hours; Vercel Hobby runs once a day anywhere in 00:30-01:29 Colombo. | `src/lib/tickets/deadlines.ts`; `src/lib/cron/decide.ts` |
| 21 | Escalation | **Customer stages** (meter, payment): the owner is alerted when the ticket becomes Overdue (escalation level 1); the admin is not involved. **Owner stages** (reading review, slip check): reminders to the owner at 24 h and 48 h, the **admin** after 72 h (level 2; listed under Admin › Escalations). Payment overdue: customer reminders on days 1, 7 and 14 after the due date (the first comes with the Overdue status), plus a **weekly summary** to the owner on their summary weekday, sent once per week (a missed run catches up within the week). Disputes pause reminders. | Spec 5.4, 8.3, 9 ("owner, then admin"). | `decideTicket`; `app.mark_ticket_overdue`, `app.escalate_ticket`, `app.cron_overdue_summaries`, `app.notify_once` |
| 22 | Estimated invoice | When the meter deadline passes and the owner bills estimates (Settings, off by default): the ticket becomes Overdue (owner alerted), then the daily job creates a **draft** estimated invoice (commitment only, one cycle, customer credits taken off automatically, rule 13) and the ticket goes to Pending owner review. The owner confirms or rejects it like a reading; the system never issues it (11.1). **At most one estimate per ticket**: after a rejection the ticket waits for the reading. The next real reading measures from the last confirmed reading and credits the estimate (rule 12). | Spec 11.6. | `app.create_estimated_invoice`, `app.verify_estimated_invoice`; `buildEstimate` |
| 23 | Catch-up | A run opens **every** missed cycle in order (several missed months included), from the first billing date, never for a terminated agreement or a suspended account. A ticket's meter deadline counts from the day it actually opens, not from its cycle date, so a late run never costs the customer days. When a cycle opens while an earlier ticket of the agreement is still waiting for its reading, that earlier ticket is flagged **Overdue** (owner alerted, spec 11.6); tickets waiting for payment are not affected. | Spec 5.5, 11.6, 11.7. | `app.cron_due_agreements`, `app.open_billing_cycle(p_now)` |
| 24 | Pause on suspension | Open tickets of a suspended (or deactivated) customer or owner are **paused**: no reminders, overdue, escalation, estimate or late fee; the owner can still process them. On reactivation the ticket resumes and its deadlines move forward by the paused time; an unpaid invoice that was not yet overdue gets its due date moved forward by the paused days. Pause and resume are on the ticket's timeline. Cycles due while suspended open at the next run after reactivation. Pause and resume happen right after the status change and in every daily run. | Spec 11.3, 11.8 ("frozen", "resume on reactivation"). | `app.set_ticket_pause`, `syncPauses` (also called by `changeAccountStatus`) |
| 25 | One reading for several cycles | A reading covers every cycle since the last confirmed reading (rule 12). In the **same transaction**, the older tickets of the agreement still waiting for a reading (Meter requested, or Overdue from it) are closed as **Cancelled** with the reason "Billed in the cycle N invoice" and a ticket event, so no cycle can be billed twice; a reading on such an older ticket is then refused. A reading is also refused while an older reading of the agreement waits for the owner's review (it would bill that cycle again). The customer's Home shows **one** "Enter meter reading" task per machine, the newest ticket, saying how many months it covers. | Client addition to rule 23 (2026-10-10). Cancelled is the final status the state machine allows from the meter stage, as for returns (rule 4). | `app.submit_meter_reading` (0019); `customerHome` |
| 26 | Daily job | One run a day (Vercel Cron) plus "Run daily job now" for admins and `npm run cron:run`. Every write is a compare-and-set rpc, so a second run the same day changes nothing; a run younger than 10 minutes holds a lease and a parallel run is recorded as skipped. Work is done in batches of 50 with a 45 s budget (Vercel Hobby limit 60 s); leftover work is recorded (`remaining`) and done by the next run. Every run is in `cron_runs` (admin › Daily job). Outside production only, a request may simulate "now" (`x-rentdesk-now` or `?now=`); production builds ignore it. | CLAUDE.md idempotency rule; Vercel Hobby limits. | `src/lib/cron/daily.ts`, `src/lib/cron/time.ts`; `cron_runs` |
| 27 | Owner overrides (state machine) | Cancel needs a reason (a reading waiting for review is superseded and its photo deleted; an open dispute ends; money paid becomes a credit). Clearing an overdue payment needs a reason **and a new due date**. Answering a dispute without cancelling returns the ticket to Awaiting payment (the invoice to Partially paid if part was paid). A reopened ticket waits for the owner, who asks for the payment again; reversing the payment itself (e.g. a returned cheque) comes with the payments task. Admins never act on a ticket. | Spec 5.3, 11.1, 11.4, 11.7. | `src/lib/tickets/states.ts`, `transitions.ts`; `app.transition_ticket(p_due_date)`, `app.raise_dispute`, `app.resolve_dispute` |
| 28 | Rejection limit | After `max_meter_rejections` rejections (3 by default, a setting) the customer cannot send the reading again: the database refuses it (`TOO_MANY_REJECTIONS`), the customer's page says the rental company will enter it, and the owner is told when rejecting the last time; the owner enters it manually (rule 32). Rejecting an estimate does not count. | Spec 11.3 "after 3 rejections (configurable) the owner enters the reading manually". | `app.submit_meter_reading` (0020); `rejectReview`, `getMeterEntry` |
| 29 | Corrections | The owner can correct a customer's reading only while it waits for review. The customer's typed value is kept (`corrected_from_value`, never overwritten by a second correction) and shown to them as old → new; a note is required (shown to the customer); the billing engine recalculates the draft with the same credits the owner removed; the database re-checks it; the event, the audit log and the customer notification are written in the same transaction. The customer may dispute later (spec 11.3). | Spec 6.1, 11.3, INV-08. | `app.correct_meter_reading`; `correctReview` |
| 30 | Photo pipeline | The phone compresses the live frame (longest side 1600 px, JPEG quality stepped down) to about 200 KB; the bucket's 1 MB is the hard cap. The browser uploads it to `{owner}/{ticket}/{photo id}.jpg` (storage RLS: own ticket, waiting for a reading); the server checks the folder, the size and the JPEG signature before recording it. The capture time comes from the device; received and submitted times from the server. On confirm the file is deleted at once (the daily job retries); a rejected photo is purged at resubmission or after the retention days; files uploaded but never submitted are purged after 2 days. | CLAUDE.md rule 5, spec 6.5, INV-03, INV-14. | `src/lib/meter/compress.ts`, `checkPhoto`; `app.cron_orphan_photos` |
| 31 | Draft on the device | The typed readings and the submission's idempotency key are kept in the browser per ticket and cleared after a successful send, so a refresh or a lost connection loses nothing and a retry never creates a second submission. The photo is never stored on the device: after a refresh the customer takes it again. | INV-13; client privacy. | `src/lib/meter/draft.ts` |
| 32 | Manual entry | The owner can type the reading whenever the ticket waits for one (also overdue, or after the rejection limit). No photo; a reason is required and shown to the customer; same billing engine; audited (`MANUAL_ENTRY` event). The owner then reviews and confirms it like any reading. | INV-12, spec 11.2 (no camera), 11.6. | `enterReadingForCustomer`; `app.submit_meter_reading(OWNER_MANUAL)` |
| 33 | Language | **RentDesk is English only** (client decision, 2026-10-11). Names and addresses are typed in English letters. Every field that can appear on an invoice or its PDF (company name, address and bank details; customer name, business name and address; machine brand, model and serial number; installation location; payment instructions) accepts printable ASCII, line breaks and the curly quotes, dashes and ellipsis phone keyboards insert; anything else is refused by Zod on the server with "Please use English letters only." (shown under the field). Defence in depth: if an unsupported character still reaches the PDF (old data, a direct write), it is printed as "?" and a warning is logged; the PDF never fails or shows broken text. All PDF text uses one embedded font, Noto Sans Regular and Bold (OFL). Sinhala/Tamil can be added later with HarfBuzz shaping: see the [spike findings](spikes/pdf-sinhala-tamil.md) (pdf-lib alone draws them broken). | Client decision after the spike showed pdf-lib cannot shape Sinhala. | `src/lib/text/english.ts`; schemas in `src/lib/{branding,accounts,machines,agreements,invoices}`; `sanitizeDocument` |
| 34 | Invoice PDF versions | A PDF is made when an invoice is issued (meter confirm, estimate confirm, machine return) and a **new version** whenever the invoice changes in a way the PDF shows: late fee added, due date changed, amounts or credits changed, cancelled (with a CANCELLED stamp). Payment status changes make **no** new version (no PAID stamp; payment proof is a separate receipt PDF in the payments task). Old versions are kept (`v1.pdf`, `v2.pdf`, …); the newest is the invoice's PDF. The same content never makes a second version (content hash). Customer and machine details are fixed by version 1, the company details by the invoice's branding snapshot (BRD-03). A database trigger marks the PDF pending, so no change is missed; the server renders it at once and, if that fails, the invoice stays issued, the customer sees "Invoice PDF is being prepared" and the daily job retries (last step, so late fees of the same run are included). A render claims the invoice for 5 minutes and is READY only if nothing changed while it ran. | INV-09, BRD-03; spec 11.4 (cancel and reissue). | `app.invoice_pdf_flag`, `claim_/record_invoice_pdf` (0022-0023); `src/lib/invoices/pdf-job.ts`; daily job |
| 35 | Branding files | Logos and letterheads are stored at paths made from their SHA-256 (`{owner}/logos/{hash}.png`, `{owner}/letterheads/{hash}.{pdf,jpg,png}`) and are **never overwritten or deleted**; "Remove" only clears the company profile. An invoice's branding snapshot pins the exact files and the layout, so a later version of an old invoice looks like the first. The company profile and the branding bucket are written by the server only (after validation); owners no longer write them directly. | BRD-03 (issued invoices never change); client agreed to keep removed files. | `src/lib/branding/files.ts`; migration 0022 |
| 36 | Letterhead | An A4 JPG/PNG or a PDF of exactly 1 page, at most 5 MB, type from the magic bytes; password-protected or unreadable PDFs are refused. A file that is not A4 portrait (more than 3 % off) is accepted with a warning and **stretched to A4**. Images are re-encoded (EXIF rotation applied, metadata dropped, at most A4 at 300 dpi). Layout presets (percent of the page, left/top/width/height): Header only 7/20/86/76, Header and footer 7/20/86/64, Full-page design 12/24/76/56; or the owner drags the area (at least 50 % × 40 % of the page). The invoice data shrinks to fit the area (down to 75 %), then continues on more pages with the letterhead repeated. On a letterhead the built-in company block is not drawn (the letterhead carries it); the footer still names the company, invoice, version and page. The sample preview uses the template on screen before it is saved. | BRD-04..07; spec gives no limits. | `src/lib/branding/letterhead.ts`, `src/lib/invoices/pdf/layout.ts`, `render.ts` |
| 37 | Camera safety | The shutter is enabled only when the video is really playing: `loadeddata`, a real size and 3 rendered frames (15 s, then "The camera did not start"). The captured frame is checked on a 64 × 48 copy: average brightness below 28 (too dark), above 235 (washed out) or brightness spread below 8 (one flat colour, blank) is refused with "The photo came out too dark or blank — please retake it."; the camera stays open and the photo never reaches the form, so it cannot be sent. | Found on a real phone: an early capture gave a black frame. | `src/lib/meter/frame-check.ts`; `camera-capture.tsx` |
| 38 | PDF downloads | "Download PDF" goes to `/api/invoices/{id}/pdf` (`?v=n` for a version): the signed-in user's own client finds the file and signs a **60-second** URL, so the table and storage RLS decide (owner: own tenant; customer: own issued invoices; admin: all), and the browser is redirected to it in a new tab. Owners and admins see every version with its reason. The sample preview is stored at `{owner}/preview/sample.pdf` (only the owner can read it) with a 5-minute URL. | CLAUDE.md rule 6 (private buckets, short-lived signed URLs). | `src/app/api/invoices/[id]/pdf/route.ts`; `src/lib/invoices/template.ts` |
| 39 | Payment split | One payment may cover several bills of one customer (one bank transfer for several bills). Within the bills the customer ticks, it pays the **oldest due date first** (no due date last; ties by invoice number), each up to what is left on it; bills the amount does not reach are left out of the slip; **what is left over becomes a customer credit** (kind Overpayment; Advance when the owner records money with no bill), taken off the next invoices (rule 13). When the owner accepts a smaller amount (less arrived), the same rule runs on that amount. | Client decision (spec 8 gives no rule). Oldest first clears the bills that are overdue first. | `planAllocation` (`src/lib/payments/allocation.ts`) = `app.plan_allocation` (DB test) |
| 40 | Duplicate slips | A slip is flagged **Possible duplicate** when an earlier payment of the same owner has the same file (SHA-256 of the stored file, or of the file as the customer chose it, before the phone made it smaller) or the same bank reference (case and spaces ignored) **and** the same amount. The customer is warned before sending and may send it anyway; the owner sees the flag and the earlier payment. Never a block. Another customer's payment is not described to the customer. | PAY-11, spec 11.5 "duplicate warning shown to the owner"; a reference alone repeats (standing orders), so the amount must match too. | `findDuplicate` = `app.payment_duplicates` |
| 41 | Slip files | Gallery, files and the camera are all allowed for slips (unlike meter photos). JPG, PNG or PDF up to 5 MB. Pictures over 2 MB (or HEIC) are made smaller on the phone (JPEG, longest side 2400 px); PDFs are sent as they are. The browser uploads to the customer's own folder `{owner}/{customer}/{id}.{ext}`; the server then checks the stored bytes: type by magic bytes, size, a picture that really decodes, a PDF that opens without a password, has at most 10 pages and no JavaScript, launch actions, embedded files, rich media or XFA. A refused file is deleted. The owner sees only recorded slips. Files never sent are purged after 2 days. Slips are kept `payment_slip_retention_days` (7 years by default; the purge after that is not built yet). **A real virus scan needs a paid or hosted scanner: deferred** (client agreed). | PAY-03, CLAUDE.md rule 6, spec 8.2; Vercel's 4.5 MB request limit means the browser uploads directly. | `slipPlan`, `checkSlipFile`; storage policies (0025) |
| 42 | Ticket follows the money | Every payment change moves each ticket only along the allowed transitions, with one event per step: money added → (Reopened →) (Awaiting payment →) Payment submitted → Closed or Partially paid; a slip rejected → Awaiting payment (or Partially paid); money taken back → a closed ticket is Reopened and goes to Awaiting payment, a partly paid one with nothing left paid goes to **Awaiting payment (the one new edge)**, otherwise the status stays and a Payment event is written. An owner-recorded payment therefore shows two steps (submitted, then closed). A reversed bill that is past its due date becomes Overdue at the next daily run. | Spec 5.3 / section 11 statuses stay exact; one place for every payment path. | `app.settle_invoice_payment`, `app.ticket_hop`; `states.ts` (`reversePayment`, `paymentWithdrawn`, `reallocatePayment`) |
| 43 | Reversal and reallocation | The owner can **reverse** an accepted payment (returned cheque, transfer that never arrived) or **move** it to other bills of the customer (applied to the wrong bill), each with a required reason, audited and the customer told. Reversal: the bills owe the money again, unused credits it made become **VOID**, the receipt is marked **REVERSED** (new PDF version, stamp). Moving: the payment is split again over the chosen bills (rule 39), its credit recalculated, the receipt keeps its number and gets a new version. Both are **refused** while a credit the payment made is used (even by a draft: remove it there first) or refunded, while one of its bills has a slip waiting, is disputed or cancelled, and for payments from a security deposit. | TKT-10, spec 11.5 (client asked for reallocation now). Money records are never silently changed (11.1). | `app.reverse_payment`, `app.reallocate_payment`, `app.assert_payment_credits_unused` |
| 44 | Receipts | Every accepted payment gets a receipt (slips, owner-recorded payments, advances, security deposit deductions; earlier payments were given one by 0025). Numbers are per owner, `RCT-` and 6 digits, taken in the accepting transaction, so unique and gap-free. A receipt prints the amount received (and the slip amount if different), date paid, method, reference, each bill with what it paid and what was left, the credit kept, the customer's balance at that moment and the company branding (same frame and letterhead as invoices, decision 36).
