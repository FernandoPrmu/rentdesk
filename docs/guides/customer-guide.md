# RentDesk — Customer guide

Your photocopy machine rental company uses RentDesk. Once a month you send a photo of the machine's meter. Then you get
your bill on your phone, and you can pay and send the payment slip from your phone too. You can also ask for service.

---

## 1. First sign-in

1. Open the link your rental company gave you and tap **Sign in**.
2. Type the **username** and **temporary password** they gave you.
3. Choose your own new password (at least 10 characters, with a letter and a number) and tap **Save new password**.
4. If you forget your password, ask your rental company to reset it.

## 2. Put RentDesk on your phone

Then you can open it like any other app.

**Android (Chrome)**

1. Sign in. When the card **Install RentDesk on this phone** appears, tap **Install**.
2. Or: tap the menu (three dots, top right), then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

**iPhone (Safari)**

1. Open RentDesk in **Safari** and sign in.
2. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.

The steps are also under **Help › Install the app**.

## 3. Send your meter reading (every month)

Your **Home** screen always shows **What you need to do now**. When a reading is due you will see
**Enter meter reading**. You also get a message by app and email.

1. Tap **Enter meter reading**.
2. Tap **Open camera**. Allow the camera when your phone asks.
3. Point the camera at the machine's meter screen. Make sure the numbers are sharp and fill the frame.
   Tap **Take photo**. If it is blurry or dark, tap **Retake photo**.
4. Type the number(s) the meter shows:
   - Black and white machine: one number.
   - Colour machine: the **B&W** number and the **Colour** number. Do **not** type the **Total** number.
     Tap **Which numbers do I type?** to see an example.
5. Check the amount RentDesk shows, then tap **Send reading**.
6. Wait for **"Reading sent"**. If the connection is slow, RentDesk keeps your numbers on the phone. Tap
   **Send reading** again: it is never sent twice.

Please send it within 4 days. RentDesk reminds you.

If your rental company sends the reading back, you will see why. Take a new photo and send it again.

Only a live photo works. You cannot choose a picture from your gallery.

**Camera does not work?** RentDesk shows step-by-step help. You can also write to your rental company in the ticket's
messages; they can type the reading for you.

## 4. Pay your bill

When your reading is confirmed you get the bill (with a PDF) by app and email.

1. Go to **Bills** to see what you owe. Tap a bill to see its details or **Download PDF**.
2. Pay your rental company by bank transfer, cash deposit or cheque. Their bank details are on the bill.
3. In RentDesk tap **Pay this bill** (or **Send a payment slip** on Home).
4. Tick the bill(s) you paid, check the amount, type the date you paid, choose how you paid and type the bank reference.
5. Add the slip: take a photo, or choose a picture or PDF of the slip.
6. Tap **Send payment slip**.

Your rental company checks that the money arrived. Then you get a **receipt**. All your payments and receipts are under
**Bills › Payments**.

If you paid more than the bill, the extra money is kept as a **credit** and taken off your next bill.

## 5. Ask for service (repair, toner, parts)

1. Tap **Request service** (on Home, under **Machines**, or under **Help**).
2. Choose the machine and the type: **Breakdown / repair**, **Toner**, **Spare part**, **Maintenance** or **Other**.
   For toner on a colour machine, tick the colours you need.
3. Describe the problem and choose how urgent it is.
4. Optional: add a photo of the problem.
5. Tap **Send request**.

Follow it under **Service**. You get a message at every step.

When the work is done, RentDesk asks **Is it fixed?**

- Tap **Yes, it is fixed** to close it, then give a rating if you like.
- Tap **No, still not fixed** and say what is wrong. The request goes back to your rental company.

## 6. Do you think a bill is wrong?

1. Open the bill under **Bills**.
2. Tap **Dispute this bill**.
3. Explain what is wrong. You can add a photo or a PDF (for example a photo of the meter).
4. Tap **Send dispute**.

While the dispute is open you get no reminders and no late fee for that bill. Your rental company answers in RentDesk:
they may correct the bill, give a credit, or explain why the bill is right. They may also ask you for a fresh meter
photo; it then appears on your Home.

You can dispute a bill once. For anything else, write in the ticket's **messages**.

## 7. Messages and settings

- The **bell** at the top shows new messages. Tap it to read them, or tap **Mark all as read**.
- **Help › Email settings**: turn reminder or update emails off. Bills, receipts and account emails always come.
- **Help › Change password** and **Sign out**. Sign out when you use a shared phone; RentDesk then removes everything
  it kept on the phone.

## 8. If something goes wrong

- **"No internet connection"**: check your mobile data or Wi-Fi. The page opens again by itself.
- **"New version available — tap to refresh"**: tap it. You stay on the same page.
- **"We could not confirm…"**: read the message. If it says "Tap … again: it is never sent twice", just tap again.
- **Questions about your bill or machine**: contact your rental company. Their phone and email are under **Help**.
