# RentDesk — Business rule decisions

Rules the spec (`docs/spec.md` v1.1) does not define, chosen during development. Please
review each one; any of them can be changed. "Where" points to the code or setting that
implements it.

**Client review (2026-10-09):** rules 1, 2, 4, 5, 7 and 13 were changed and rules 16–19 added
(advance payments and security deposits). The new requirement IDs are in `docs/spec.md`
section 18.

**Task 5 (2026-10-10):** rules 20-27 (tickets and the daily job). The client agreed rules 20-24 and 27
as proposed, and rule 23 with the addition that became rule 25.

**Task 6a (2026-10-11):** rules 28-32 (meter entry and the owner's review), agreed by the client as proposed.

**Task 6b (2026-10-11):** rules 33-38 (invoice PDFs, letterhead, camera safety). The client decided rule 33
(English only) and agreed rules 34-38 as proposed, including keeping removed logos and letterheads (rule 35) and no
PAID stamp on invoice PDFs (rule 34; proof of payment will be a separate receipt PDF in the payments task).

**Task 7 (2026-10-11):** rules 39-46 (payments). The client agreed the split (rule 39), the ageing of bills with a
slip waiting (rule 45), adding reallocation now (rule 43) and leaving the virus scan for later (rule 41); the other
rules are proposed for review.

**Task 8 (2026-10-11):** rules 47-56 (disputes, ticket messages, corrections after confirmation, meter baselines, the
section 11 pass; `docs/scenarios.md`). The client approved one dispute per invoice (rule 47), cancel-and-reissue
corrections (rule 49), carrying the old meter's copies (rule 51), the fresh meter photo (rule 55), dispute reminders on
day 3 and 5 with the admin on day 7 (rule 52), logging denied access (rule 54) and the missed-run alert after 30 hours
(rule 53); the other rules are proposed for review.

**Task 9 (2026-10-11):** rules 57-64 (service requests). The client chose the optional charge on the next bill
(rule 59), an optional discount for breakdowns (rule 60), the urgent times for URGENT and HIGH (rule 61), and the
owner told at once with URGENT and HIGH highlighted (rule 62), and approved the rules as described in the plan.
Rule 60 was planned as a negative ADJUSTMENT line on the next bill and built as a customer credit instead, because
a negative line could make the next bill negative and then block the customer's reading or the machine's return
(the engine refuses a negative bill); the client approved the credit (2026-10-11). Rule 64 (the owner's cost hidden
from customers) was added while building.

**Task 10 (2026-10-11):** rules 65-76 (notifications and email; rule 76 and the sender lease in rule 65 came from the verification on 2026-10-12). The client approved them as planned, with one change:
the owner is emailed when a new service request is raised (category Updates, URGENT/HIGH first in the subject), while the
customer's service-request updates stay in-app only (rule 66). The meter request (TKT-01) is essential; templates cover
email only with defaults in code (rule 71); the bounce webhook is included, off until its secret is set (rule 67).

**Task 11 (2026-10-12):** rules 77-86 (dashboards, reports, exports, settings, support view, agreement end reminders). The
client decided: subscription plans deferred (rule 85), platform branding stays in code (rule 85), Excel through an
established library (write-excel-file) rather than a hand-made writer (rule 83), the income definitions in rule 77 (with
the deposit-refund point below), an optional support-view reason (rule 81) and the agreement end reminder now (rule 86).
**Deposit refunds (rule 77), approved by the client on 2026-10-15:** the first definition said "minus credit refunds and
deposit refunds". Since a security deposit received is not income, subtracting its refund would count it as a loss (deposit
Rs. 10,000: Rs. 3,000 pays bills, Rs. 5,000 refunded, Rs. 2,000 kept → the owner keeps Rs. 5,000, but subtracting the refund
would show Rs. 0). Collected therefore subtracts **credit refunds only**; deposit refunds count as neither income nor loss.
